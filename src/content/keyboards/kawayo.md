I won this Kawayo by The Flying Penguin at MYMK 2025. It’s a 65% CNC aluminum board with a brass Toblerone rear weight.

My build pairs Kailh BCP switches with clone GMK Jamon keycaps, Typeplus × YIKB stabilizers, and a custom-cut POM plate. It uses a PCBSnap mount and runs without foam.
