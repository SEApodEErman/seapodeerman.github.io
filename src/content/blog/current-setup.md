This is the current state of my everyday setup: the PC I use, the audio gear I reach for, and the keyboards that keep the desk interesting. I will update this note as parts of the setup change.

## PC

- **CPU:** AMD Ryzen 5 5500
- **RAM:** 32GB (16GB × 2) at 3200MHz
- **GPU:** NVIDIA RTX 5060 (8GB GDDR7)
- **Monitor:** ASUS TUF VG259QM5A (24.5" 1920x1080 240hz)
- **Mouse:** Fantech Helios II Pro

## Audio gear

- **Philips SHP9500:** daily driver at the desk
- **Moondrop Aria 2 Red:** daily on-the-road driver
- **Moondrop May**
- **Tangzu Wan’er**

The SHP9500 is the usual choice at home, while the Aria 2 Red is the pair I bring with me. The May and Wan’er stay in the rotation when I feel like changing things up.
