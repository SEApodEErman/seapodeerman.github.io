Mapping requests can turn a short conversation into a small project. osu! ReqTrac gives the links, notes, deadlines, and people involved a place to stay together.

It’s a cross-platform desktop app for hitsounds, guest difficulties, storyboards, and custom request categories. It is released and still in its early stages, with more features planned.

## A queue with context

Requests carry statuses, priorities, deadlines, tags, notes, history, and requester information. Search, filters, and bulk actions help keep the list manageable.

## Beatmap information close by

Connecting an osu! account through OAuth lets the app sync beatmap metadata, covers, difficulties, and user information. Useful context is cached locally.

## Room for other work

Manual and non-osu! requests can live alongside beatmap requests. Custom categories keep the workspace useful beyond the default types.
