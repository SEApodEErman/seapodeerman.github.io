Kagome is a personal favourite of mine. The song stuck with me, and I’m really happy with how the map turned out.

A lot of my time in the editor is spent listening closely and trying things out. This is one of the maps I keep coming back to when I want to share what I enjoy making.

## Music into patterns

I care about the small choices that make a map feel coherent: how a rhythm takes shape, how a pattern follows the song, and how it feels to play.

## Try the map

The beatmap set is on osu!, where you can find the song, difficulties, and download.
