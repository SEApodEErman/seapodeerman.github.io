I went all-out on hitsounds for this one, choosing samples to mirror the song’s multi-genre shifts. Possibly one of my best work til this date even if it's relatively simple to others. (Do note that I spedran the entire hitsounds in a few hours at work.)

Alongside the sound work, I solo-mapped the Hard difficulty and contributed sections to the Easy, Expert, and top difficulty.

## Following the genre changes

The song moves through different styles, so the hitsounds needed to move with it. Sample selection was a big part of giving those shifts their own texture.

## My part in the set

The Hard is my solo mapping contribution. On the Easy, Expert, and top difficulty, my work is part of a shared map.
